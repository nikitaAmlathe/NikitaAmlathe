Thought Leadership
Operational Learnings
Observations from 9+ years of hands-on supply chain execution across inventory governance, operations, and process discipline.
Inventory Governance
System Stock ≠ Actual Stock — Always Verify
The most dangerous assumption in inventory management is that your SAP balance reflects physical reality. Regular cycle counts, bin-level verification, and consumption reconciliation are not optional — they are the foundation of supply chain reliability.
Operations vs Planning
Strategies Built on Paper Don't Always Work on the Ground
The gap between a planned process and an executable one is often where operations fail. SOPs must be designed by people who have stood on the shop floor — not just written in conference rooms. Ground validation before rollout is non-negotiable.
Audit Readiness
Audit Readiness is a Daily Practice, Not a Pre-Audit Rush
Zero audit non-conformance is not achieved by preparing before an audit — it is achieved by maintaining process discipline every single day. When compliance is embedded in daily operations, audits become confirmations, not stress events.
Greenfield Execution
Building from Zero Teaches You What Really Matters
When you commission a warehouse from scratch, you learn very quickly which processes are essential and which are decorative. Greenfield execution forces operational thinking at its most fundamental level.
Workforce & Compliance
GMP/HIRA Compliance Starts with the First Day of Training
Compliance cannot be enforced after the fact — it must be built into the team from day one. When people understand why a process exists, they follow it consistently — even without supervision.
Cost Optimisation
Sustainable Cost Reduction Comes from Process Design, Not Shortcuts
Cutting costs by reducing steps creates downstream problems. Sustainable savings — like the 15% achieved at Coca-Cola and Amazon — come from eliminating waste in processes: redundant steps, inefficient routes, uncontrolled material movement.
